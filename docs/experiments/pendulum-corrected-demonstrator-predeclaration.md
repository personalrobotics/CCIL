# Pendulum corrected-demonstrator study: predeclaration, version 1 (#143)

**Status: version 1, binding when merged.** This must be merged together with revision 2 of `docs/experiments/pendulum-eval-v2-predeclaration.md`, before either historical Part B or the corrected controller is run. The design therefore cannot react to the historical fresh-start results. No corrected demonstration has been generated, and no policy has been trained on one.

## Why

The historical Pendulum demonstrations were generated by a controller with a wrapping inconsistency (revision 2 of the evaluation predeclaration; `docs/fixtures/evidence/pendulum-eval-v2/expert-diagnosis.json`). It tested the LQR window on the angle wrapped to [0, 2π), but used the raw atan2 angle in the LQR term, so it saturates at +3 on the θ > π side of upright. This study regenerates the demonstrations with that one defect removed. It then reruns BC and CCIL and compares the result with the historical study, paired.

**Question.** With a demonstrator that applies its own stated control law consistently, how do BC and CCIL behave under the task-aligned metrics, and does the CCIL − BC difference change from the historical study?

## What has been seen

- Everything listed in the evaluation predeclaration, including the failed reconstruction and its diagnosis.
- **The corrected controller at the historical states only.** It is the primary variant of the failed reconstruction's check 1 (wrapped angle for both window and action, cosine of that angle). So its action at every historical demonstration state has been computed and compared with the stored action.
- **Nothing closed-loop.** No closed-loop trajectory of the corrected controller has been computed, and no corrected demonstration, policy or score exists.

## The corrected demonstrator

`tools/pendulum_eval/expert.py`, `act(states)` with its defaults (the primary variant), unchanged from the frozen reconstruction commit bb18bf0:

- θ = atan2(sin θ, cos θ), wrapped to [0, 2π), and δ = θ − π. **The same δ is used in the window test and in the LQR action.**
- LQR, when |δ| < 0.1: a = −20.11 δ − 7.08 θ̇. These are the printed gains, retained as printed.
- Energy shaping otherwise: a = −θ̇(½θ̇² − 9.81 cos θ − 9.81), with cos θ the cosine of the wrapped angle. This branch is retained.
- The action is clamped to [−3, 3] (retained).
- **Evaluated in float64.**

**Action data contract.**
- The action is computed and clamped in float64, then cast once to float32.
- **That float32 value is both the action applied to the simulator and the action stored.** Every stored transition (s_t, a_t, s_{t+1}) is therefore exactly what the dynamics produced.
- The pre-cast float64 value is kept only as a diagnostic, in the generation report and not in the training dataset. The report gives its largest difference from the applied action.

**Controller acceptance check** (behavioral, before any generation): the control law is odd-symmetric about upright. For synthetic states mirrored across θ = π, a(π + δ, −θ̇) = −a(π − δ, θ̇) to 1e-12, on a grid of δ in [−0.2, 0.2] and θ̇ in [−3, 3] that crosses the switch. The historical controller fails this check on the θ > π side. Its outcome is reported, and a failure stops the study.

**No performance threshold, and no tuning.** The controller is fixed above. Its closed-loop behavior is reported as an outcome and never used to change it.

## Corrected demonstrations

| Property | Value, the same as the historical data |
|---|---|
| Initial states | each historical episode's stored first state (float64), in the same order |
| Episodes | 100 continuous, 500 discontinuous |
| Timestep | 0.02 s |
| Horizon | 500 state frames and 500 actions per episode |
| Dynamics | v6's scalar generator step (`tools/m3_pendulum/env.step`: scipy RK45, legacy semantics), with the task's velocity flips |
| Applied and stored action | the same float32 value: the float64 clamped action cast once (action data contract above) |
| Storage | `ccil.trajectories` 1.0, with states as float64 and actions as float32, the historical dataset's dtypes, so the training pipeline sees the same schema |

- **Generation.** A committed script generates each dataset deterministically. Each dataset is recorded by its SHA-256 and regenerated identically on macOS and Linux (bitwise, or to a declared cross-host tolerance if the integrator differs across hosts).
- **Transition replay check.**
  - Every generated transition is replayed from its stored state s_t and stored action a_t through the same generator step, with the task's discontinuity, and must recover the stored s_{t+1}.
  - On the generating host it must match exactly (bitwise). On the other host, it must match within 1e-12 absolute in every coordinate.
  - Any failure stops the study before training, and is reported.
- **Datasets are recorded by hash.** They are not committed, as with the historical conversion.
- **Demonstrator report** (descriptive), paired per episode with the historical demonstrations on the same starts:
  - v2 occupancy, height, reach, final hold, and the behavior diagnostics;
  - #143's six gate checks, evaluated and reported. They do not gate this study, because nothing here may be tuned.

## Training

- **Arms:** BC and CCIL only, with v6's §5 definitions, paper settings and shared policy settings, unchanged. The CCIL arm's labels, action noise, support radius, dynamics model and balancing are the task configuration's.
- **No tuning** of anything.
- **Only the dataset changes.** The pipeline is v6's study tooling, run from the frozen-tooling commit d4ef5d1. If that tooling cannot take a different dataset without a code change, the minimal change is made in a separate reviewed commit before any corrected-data training, and the change is reported.
- **Training seeds 1201–1220,** the same as the historical checkpoints, so each corrected model is paired with the historical model of the same seed and arm.

**Stage-identity preflight** (before any corrected-data training). Each stage's run ID hashes the SHA-256 of every input artifact (`src/ccil/pipelines/stages.py`). The preflight shows that no historical artifact can be reused:
- The corrected run root is a new, empty directory, separate from v6's.
- For every unit (task × arm × seed), `data.prepare` runs on the corrected dataset. Its identity's input hash must equal the corrected dataset's SHA-256 and differ from the historical dataset's.
- Every corrected `data.prepare` run ID, and the hash of its prepared artifact, must differ from every `data.prepare` run ID and artifact hash in the v6 records. Because every downstream identity hashes its upstream artifacts, distinct prepared data gives distinct dynamics, label and policy identities.
- **After training, a provenance audit** checks every corrected unit's chain: `data.prepare`, then dynamics, labels and policy. Every input hash must trace back to the corrected dataset, and no run ID or artifact hash may appear in the v6 records.
- Any failure stops the study and is reported.

## Evaluation

- **Starts:** identical to historical Part B. Training seed s is evaluated on 100 episodes from `SeedSequence([s + 1000, 7])`, which gives evaluation seeds 2201–2220.
- **Evaluator:** the batched evaluator, with v6's dynamics, horizon, unclipped action semantics and discontinuity.
- **Metric:** `pendulum_swingup_v2` (`pendulum_eval.profiles/1`). Upright occupancy is primary. Height, final hold and the behavior diagnostics are secondary.
- **Historical results:** taken unchanged from historical Part B (the same checkpoints and the same starts). Nothing historical is re-evaluated here.
- **Zero action** on the same starts is reported, unchanged from Part B.
- **The corrected demonstrator** is also scored on these starts as a descriptive reference for this study only. It is not a reference for historical Part B.

## Statistics

The interval is v6's union of BCa and paired t, with v6's resampling settings, over n = 20 training seeds, paired by seed. Per task there are two primary analyses, so c = 1 − 0.05/4 = 0.9875 (Bonferroni over 2 tasks × 2 analyses).

1. **Corrected CCIL − BC** in upright occupancy.
2. **Paired change in the CCIL − BC difference:** [CCIL − BC]corrected − [CCIL − BC]historical, by seed.
   - This estimates the effect of replacing the demonstrations on the entire CCIL-versus-BC pipeline: dynamics fits, labels and policies.
   - It is not the isolated causal effect of the wrapping defect. The replacement also changes the controller's evaluation precision, and every trajectory downstream of the first changed action.

**Outcomes** use v6's rules: "improves" or "increases" when the lower endpoint is > 0, "worse" or "decreases" when the upper endpoint is < 0, and otherwise "no detectable difference". There is no positive control, because the study trains BC and CCIL only. A null outcome is therefore reported with its interval and no claim of absence.

**Secondary** (descriptive, 95%, no decisions):
- corrected − historical for each arm, paired by seed;
- the secondary metrics for every comparison above;
- the demonstrator report;
- noise robustness, with the observation-only and action-only channels separated at v6's levels, as in Part B.

**Failures and exclusions:** as v6 §9. Non-finite actions are algorithmic failures, and there are no other exclusions.
